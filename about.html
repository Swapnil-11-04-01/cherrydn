This is Cherry DN. My work lives somewhere between memory and distortion, intimacy and distance, beauty and decay. I return often to the body, water, desire, identity and the strange ways we preserve what was never meant to stay.

I am a voice, a collector of moments that cut too deep to forget. I make music for the things I can’t say out loud. I create visuals for the versions of me that never got to live.

My debut poetry collection, The View From Above, brings these worlds together: a body of work built from fragments, confession, memory and the things that survive beneath the surface.

Cherry is the one who feels. DN is the one who watches.
