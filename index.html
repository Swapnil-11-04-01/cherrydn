The Artist

Cherry DN is a multidisciplinary artist working at the meeting point of word, sound and image — where a poem becomes a song, a song becomes a film, and a film becomes a way of remembering.

Her work moves like water over embers — unhurried, low-lit, warm beneath the surface. Through poetry collections, original music, photography and short film, she traces one long story — a becoming, told in elements.

“I don’t separate the disciplines.
They are one voice, wearing different dresses.”
02

Poetry — the Elements

Read it the way she lived it — element by element.
Four stages. One becoming.

  1. Iὕδωρ

    Water

    dissolving — grief, memory, surrender

    “I practiced drowning
    until the sea mistook me
    for one of her own.”

  2. IIπῦρ

    Fire

    igniting — desire, anger, awakening

    “Even underwater
    something in me
    refused to go out.”

  3. IIIγῆ

    Earth

    rooting — healing, body, belonging

    “The earth took my ashes
    without asking
    what they had been.”

  4. IVἀήρ

    Air

    releasing — forgiveness, flight, return

    “What the water kept,
    what the fire spared —
    I exhale, and it is sky.”
